SMBX/ABOUT

A new firm. Decades of execution.

smbx.ai was built to give buyers in the lower middle market something they have rarely had access to — a dedicated corporate development function, on demand, exclusively on their side of the table.

WHY WE BUILT IT

Over two decades as a buyer and integrator.

01
The seller always has someone.

Sellers have had professional representation for a hundred years — a broker, an advisor, a banker working their number.

02
The buyer usually doesn't.

A corporate development team is a luxury of scale. Almost nobody in the lower middle market has one, so the biggest transaction of a career gets run with no one on that side of the table.

03
So we became that team.

Sourcing, evaluation, negotiation, integration — a complete corp-dev function, led by senior dealmakers, pointed at one side of the table. Yours.

WHAT WE BELIEVE

Four convictions run the practice.

I
The buyer deserves a corner.

Sellers have had professional representation for a hundred years. In the lower middle market, buyers mostly haven't. That's the gap we exist to close.

II
Coverage wins.

The best target is usually not for sale. You find it by covering the whole market — every operator in the segment, not just the companies being shown around.

III
Focus beats breadth.

We advise buyers. Only buyers. One client per target. It makes us less flexible and far more useful.

IV
The deal isn't done at close.

Value is made or lost in the first six months after the wire. We built the integration playbook that proves it, and we stay through it.
