## Part 1 — Publicly traded strategics

### 1.1 APi Group Corporation (NYSE: APG)

The single most important disclosure set in the industry. Sub-verticals: **SV1 + SV2 + SV3** (spans all three).

**FY2025 results** (`Disclosed`, APi Group IR press release, 26 Feb 2026):
- Net revenues FY2025: **$7,911 million**. Exact wording: *"Net revenues increased by 12.7% (7.9% organic)"* versus $7,018 million in 2024.
- **Safety Services segment net revenues FY2025: $5,456 million**, 13.7% reported growth, 6.7% organic.
- Specialty Services segment net revenues FY2025: $2,460 million, 10.4% reported and organic.
- Consolidated adjusted EBITDA: **$1,041 million**, 13.2% margin, 50 bps expansion.
- **Safety Services segment earnings margin: 16.8%**, up 90 bps. Specialty Services: 10.7%, down 70 bps.
- Growth attributed verbatim to *"inspection, service, and monitoring revenues, strong growth in project revenues, acquisitions, and pricing improvements."*
- FY2025 acquisitions: **$186 million net cash outlay** (`Disclosed`).

> ⚠ **Reconciliation flag, not averaged:** $5,456M + $2,460M = $7,916M, against a stated total of $7,911M — a **$5 million** gap presumed to be intersegment eliminations. I did **not** verify the elimination line in the 10-K. Both figures are reported here as filed; do not net them silently.

**Q1 2026 results** (`Disclosed`, Businesswire, 30 Apr 2026):
- Net revenues **$2.0 billion**, *"year-over-year growth of 15.3%, 10.4% on an organic basis."*
- Safety Services **$1,415 million** (+11.7% YoY); Specialty Services **$569 million** (+25.6% YoY).
- Adjusted EBITDA **$235 million**, margin +70 bps to 11.9%.
- FY2026 guidance **raised**: net revenues **$8,475–$8,675 million**; adjusted EBITDA **$1,150–$1,210 million**.
- CEO **Russ Becker**, verbatim: the company *"closed the CertaSite acquisition and signed transactions for Wtech and Onyx, representing an investment of more than $1 billion across these three acquisitions."*

**The inspection-first disclosure — the most load-bearing number in the stream** (`Disclosed`, APi Group PDF release, 10 Dec 2025). On the CertaSite deal APi stated the acquisition:
> *"shifts mix towards 60% of revenues from inspection, service, and monitoring"*

and that *"CertaSite's inspection-first business model aligns with our inspection-first flywheel strategy."* This is the clearest public statement of the ITM-density thesis available from any issuer in the sector.

**M&A cadence:** bolt-on strategy explicitly stated. FY2025 = $186M of cash acquisitions; Q1 2026 alone = **>$1B signed/closed across three deals** (CertaSite, Wtech, Onyx) — a step-change in cheque size, not merely cadence.

**Operating brands (partial, mixed basis):**
- **Chubb Fire & Security** — acquired from Carrier for **$3.1 billion** announced 27 Jul 2021 (`Disclosed`, Businesswire), completed 3 Jan 2022 (`Disclosed`, Businesswire).
- **Western States Fire Protection** — trades as a dba of *"APi Group Life Safety USA LLC"* (`Press-derived`, contractor-network listing; **not** verified to a filing).
- **CertaSite** — closed 2 Feb 2026 (see §2.12).
- **Wtech** and **Onyx** — named by the CEO in the Q1 2026 release; I could not verify what either business does, its geography, or its size. See `## What I could not verify`.

> **Conflict preserved:** a 2026 trade guide (CT Acquisitions, undated page, retrieved 29 Jul 2026) states APi is *"approximately $7.0B FY2025 net revenue."* APi's own 26 Feb 2026 release states **$7,911 million** for FY2025 and $7,018 million for FY2024. **Underwrite on the issuer release ($7,911M);** the $7.0B figure appears to be FY2024 mislabelled as FY2025.

### 1.5 Carrier Global Corporation (NYSE: CARR) — **complete exit from fire**

The most consequential strategic event in the sector's recent history: Carrier sold **both** of its fire businesses, creating two new sponsor-owned platforms.

**(a) Industrial Fire → Sentinel Capital Partners.** Announced 5 Mar 2024, **$1.425 billion enterprise value**, net proceeds expected to exceed $1.1 billion (`Disclosed`, Carrier corporate release, 5 Mar 2024). Brands, verbatim: *"Det-Tronics, Marioff, Autronica and Fireye."* *"approximately 1,400 employees operating in more than 20 countries."* Closed 2 Jul 2024 as **Spectrum Safety Solutions** (§2.14).

**(b) Commercial and Residential Fire → Lone Star Funds.** Closed **2 December 2024**, **$3 billion** enterprise value, *"estimated $2.2 billion net proceeds"* (`Disclosed`, Carrier corporate release, Dec 2024). Buyer described as *"an affiliate of Lone Star Funds."* Became **Kidde Global Solutions** (§2.13). Carrier called it *"the final step in our strategic portfolio transformation."*

Carrier's own summary of the programme, verbatim: it *"also included the acquisition of Viessmann Climate Solutions and the divestitures of its Industrial Fire, Access Solutions and Commercial Refrigeration businesses."*

**(c) Access Solutions (incl. LenelS2) → Honeywell** (`Press-derived`, SecurityInfoWatch). Named by Carrier as part of the same programme (`Disclosed`).

**Net position: Carrier retains no fire or life safety business.** Kidde, Edwards, Marioff, Autronica, Det-Tronics, Fireye and LenelS2 are all now under other owners. **Any consolidator register still listing Carrier as a fire OEM owner is stale.**

## Derivations

All arithmetic shown. Nothing below is sourced; everything below is computed from figures labelled `Disclosed` above.

1. **APi Safety Services share of FY2025 net revenues.** $5,456M ÷ $7,911M = 0.689673… → **68.97%**. `Estimated`. (Using the reported total, not the segment sum.)
2. **APi Specialty Services share of FY2025 net revenues.** $2,460M ÷ $7,911M = 0.310959… → **31.10%**. `Estimated`. Note (1) + (2) = 100.07%, reflecting the $5M elimination gap flagged in §1.1. **I have not reconciled it and have not adjusted either figure to force a sum of 100%.**
3. **APi segment sum vs. reported total.** $5,456M + $2,460M = $7,916M; $7,916M − $7,911M = **$5M**. `Estimated` difference; cause **not verified**.
4. **APi Safety Services implied segment earnings FY2025.** 16.8% × $5,456M = **$916.6M**. `Estimated`. (APi reports a segment *earnings* margin, not segment EBITDA; do not treat this as segment EBITDA.)
5. **APi Safety Services share of Q1 2026 revenue.** $1,415M ÷ ($1,415M + $569M) = $1,415M ÷ $1,984M = 0.713205… → **71.32%**. `Estimated`. Company states total Q1 revenue as "$2.0 billion"; the $1,984M denominator is the segment sum, so this ratio is segment-relative.
6. **Pye-Barker 2025 add-on rate.** 57 ÷ 12 = **4.75 closings per month**. `Estimated`.
7. **Pye-Barker headcount growth Jan 2025 → Mar 2026.** "nearly 8,000" → "9,000" = **≈1,000 net adds**; states 45 → 47 = **+2**. `Estimated` from two `Disclosed` datapoints ≈14 months apart. The word "nearly" makes the base imprecise; I have **not** converted this to a growth percentage.
8. **Pye-Barker average headcount per location.** 9,000 ÷ 250 = **36.0**. `Estimated`. Locations are stated as "over 250", so 36.0 is an **upper bound**, not a point estimate.
9. **Sciens add-on rate under Huron.** 13 add-ons ÷ 5 years (2016 → Dec 2021) = **2.6/yr**. `Estimated`.
10. **CertaSite add-on rate under Riverside.** 26 add-ons by Mar 2024; CertaSite's earliest announced activity is 2018 per its own newsroom → ≈6 years → **≈4.3/yr**. `Estimated`. The 2018 start is `Press-derived`, so this rate is weaker than (9).
11. **Altus add-on rate under AE Industrial.** 8 add-ons ÷ 3 years (2021 → 2024) = **≈2.7/yr**. `Estimated`.
12. **Spectrum Safety Solutions hold-to-first-carve-out.** 2 Jul 2024 → 31 Mar 2026 = **20 months**. `Estimated`.
13. **Sponsor hold periods as of 29 Jul 2026.** Pavion/Wind Point from Jun 2020 = **≈6.1 years**. Sciens/Carlyle from 15 Dec 2021 = **≈4.6 years**. `Estimated`.
14. **CertaSite implied EV/revenue — REFUSED.** APi disclosed *"more than $1 billion across these three acquisitions"* (CertaSite, Wtech, Onyx) and CertaSite FY2025 revenue of *"approximately $90 million."* Dividing $1B by $90M would produce a multiple of ≈11.1x **that is factually wrong**, because the numerator covers three businesses and the denominator one. **No CertaSite multiple is derivable from public disclosure. I did not compute one.**
15. **AI Fire and Encore implied multiples — REFUSED.** Both EVs ($1.1B, $1.8B) are `Press-derived` and neither company discloses revenue or EBITDA. **No multiple computed.**
