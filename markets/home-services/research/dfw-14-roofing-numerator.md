## Confirmed absent, with the name-collision traps that surfaced

Checked against their own rosters and not in DFW: **Baker Roofing Company**
(Raleigh), **Bone Dry Roofing** (Indianapolis), **Legacy Restoration** (MN
storm platform), **Infinity Home Services** (LightBay + Freeman Spogli), and
**Rampart Exterior Services** (Huron — Texas via Austin/San Antonio/Corpus only).

Four live collisions, recorded so the matcher never eats them:

- *Baker Roofing & Construction* (Dallas, baker-roofing.com) is an unrelated
  independent — not Baker Roofing Co. of Raleigh, and not Baker Brothers.
- *Bone Dry* — three unrelated users of the name (the Indy platform, an RCA
  brand in Georgia, and DFW locals).
- *Premier Roofing* — Aurora's Denver company (in DFW) vs Infinity Home
  Services' West Michigan brand (not in DFW). The trap was live and is closed.
- *Legacy Restoration* is not *Legacy Service Partners*. *Infinity Roofing &
  Siding* (Stronghouse) is not *Infinity Contractors* (Modigent) and not
  *Infinity Home Services*.

## The read

DFW roofing runs 907 establishments averaging 6.7 employees on $0.458B of
payroll (run 08) with a derived $2.759B of receipts on the tightest allocation
in the study (run 12) — and the metro is **overweight roofing**, at 39.58% of
Texas roofing payroll against 32.87% of plumbing/HVAC. Twelve to thirteen
platform-held nameplates against that base, half of them caveated or single
branches, is thinner ground than HVAC's eighteen parents — and the residential
storm-restoration segment, the piece the hail exposure feeds, has attracted
entries dated 2024-01, 2024-07, 2025-05, 2025-09 and 2025-10. **A consolidation
wave arriving, not arrived.**

One structural caution before this trade gets a thesis: storm-restoration
revenue is insurance-event-driven and episodic — the annuity logic that
underwrites HVAC service agreements does not transfer. Whether DFW roofing is
attractive depends on the replacement/repair mix, and no source in this pass
measures it.
