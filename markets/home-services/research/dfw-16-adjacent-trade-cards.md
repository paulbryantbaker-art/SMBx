| Residual | ≈72 in-band establishments unmatched — but see the caution | — |

**Two cautions that gate any roofing thesis.** Receipts/payroll is **6.03x**
against 3.46x for HVAC — roofing books revenue on subcontracted crews, so
employment-based dollar banding is invalid here and none is computed
(receipts/employee $442,849 is an artefact of the labour model, not
productivity). And DFW is overweight roofing (39.58% of TX payroll) because of
hail: storm-restoration revenue is episodic, not annuity. The
replacement/repair mix is unmeasured anywhere and is the deciding number.
