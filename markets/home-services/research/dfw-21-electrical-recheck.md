# Electrical, re-checked — the read sharpened, one verdict overturned

**Why this run exists.** Run 13 concluded "no residential-electrical-first PE
platform in DFW" from one pass, and recorded Milestone, Prism and Team Enoch as
notable independents. The principal's challenge triggered the second pass the
stop condition wants — run adversarially, instructed to refute.

## 1. Milestone — INDEPENDENT CONFIRMED, and structurally richer than recorded

No private-equity investment, recapitalization or platform acquisition found
across ten dated search angles. Its own pages state: *"Since 2004, Milestone has
been a family owned and operated home service company"* (callmilestone.com/about-us,
re-fetched 2026-08-03; full display name "Milestone Home Service Co.",
electrical-led, plus A/C, plumbing and air quality, offices across the
metroplex).

The new structure, from the Rockwall Economic Development Corporation's
headquarters-relocation announcement of **2026-06-09**: Milestone operates under
a family holding company, **Mpact Home Services Company**, which also owns
**Hank's Handyman Services** and **Firehouse Roofing**; Gus Antos is named
Co-Owner; the HQ is relocating to Rockwall Technology Park with 175-plus
headquarters staff. A search for Mpact or Antos with investor/PE terms returned
nothing.

**Consequences.** (a) The metro's largest electrical-led residential operator is
family-held — the strongest possible confirmation that the segment's size exists
without institutional capital. (b) **Firehouse Roofing is not independent** — a
roofing screen would have called it so. Mpact enters the register as a
family-held multi-brand parent for identification purposes.

## 2. Team Enoch — VERDICT OVERTURNED: recapitalized 2022

Run 13's "independent" verdict rested on Team Enoch's own site naming no parent.
The primary source refutes it: **McKinney Capital announced a recapitalization
of Team Enoch on 2022-03-01** — *"a rapidly growing provider of HVAC, plumbing
and electrical maintenance, repair and replacement services to residential
customers across the Dallas-Fort Worth market"*; founder Miles Hicsonmez
*"will continue as CEO and will remain a significant shareholder"*
(mckinneycapital.net release). McKinney Capital describes itself as a private,
family-owned investment firm investing exclusively in field-services companies
(Birmingham, AL). Post-recap, Team Enoch has opened Austin (Pflugerville) and
Houston (Spring) locations — platform behaviour. No subsequent resale found.

This is the marketing-copy trap named in the register's own preamble: "family
feel" copy survives a recap. The lesson generalises — **an "independent" verdict
sourced only from a company's own site is provisional by construction.**

## 3. The other findings

- **Prism Electric** — independent confirmed (founded 1994, Blake Combs, 2,000+
  employees), but **commercial/industrial** (healthcare, education, data
  centers, airports); it does not belong in a residential-electrical context.
- **IES Residential** — the public-holdco residential-electrical-first
  consolidator has a physical **DFW branch at 2500 Chandler Dr, Rowlett**
  (Dallas Builders Association member listing since 2018), new-construction
  channel. Run 13 carried IES from careers pages only; the branch is now placed.
- **Wrench Group's Baker Brothers and Berkeys sell electrical service lines**
  in DFW (their own brand pages) — plumbing/HVAC-led, restated for completeness.
- Noise checked and dismissed: Summit Park's 2025-10-06 DX Electric recap
  (Grapevine) is an electrical-parts **distributor**, not a contractor; a
  microcap's announced intent to buy an unnamed Texas electrical contractor
  (2026-01-27) is unresolved and is not evidence.

## 4. The corrected claim, exactly

**Before (run 13):** "no residential-electrical-first PE platform found in DFW."

**After (this run):** DFW residential electrical **service-and-repair** has no
dedicated consolidator. The nearest exception is one multi-trade residential
platform with an electrical-led brand — **Team Enoch, recapitalized by McKinney
Capital 2022-03-01**, since expanded to Austin and Houston. The
**new-construction** channel is consolidated by IES Residential (public
holding company, Rowlett branch). The multi-trade platforms (Wrench ×2) sell
electrical as a service line. The segment's largest operators otherwise remain
family-held — which is the finding, not its absence.

Run 13 is partially superseded by this file; the register and master carry the
corrections (A.0.5).
