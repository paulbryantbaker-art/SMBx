Parent-child summation on `RCPNCW`, exact on unrounded values: Building Equipment Contractors = electrical **$230.78 billion** + plumbing/HVAC **$267.13 billion** + other building equipment **$39.78 billion** = **$537.68 billion**; Specialty Trade Contractors **$1,131.22 billion**; Construction sector **$2,239.54 billion**.

**Findings:**
1. **"$267.1B = `RCPCWRK`, receipts for construction work" is a WRONG VARIABLE.** $267.13B is `RCPNCW`, "Net value of construction work." Actual `RCPCWRK` is **$288.33 billion**.
2. **"1,690,333 on the employment column" is a WRONG VARIABLE.** That is `HOURS` — construction/production worker annual hours in thousands. Actual `EMP` is **1,169,692** — the prior figure overstated headcount by ~44%.
3. The same `RCPNCW` mislabel applied to the $230.78 billion electrical figure.
4. Net-of-subcontracting values are **not additive across trades** the way gross values are.

**Census scope:** establishment-based; 50 states + DC only; includes receipts for construction work, additions, alterations, rehabilitation, maintenance and repairs including labor, materials and profit; excludes land cost other than site preparation, non-operating income, and work outside the 50 states + DC. Payroll and employment exclude subcontractors and their employees, and proprietors and partners of unincorporated firms.

**Vintages:** Economic Census 2022 is latest (five-year cycle; next 2027). County Business Patterns latest is **2023** (released 2025-06-26; 2024 slated for summer 2026, not yet posted). SUSB latest is **2022**.
