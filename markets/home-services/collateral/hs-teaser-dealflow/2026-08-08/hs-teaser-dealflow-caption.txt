Ninety-two HVAC services deals so far in 2026. Private equity took 47 of them.

Read one layer down and it gets more interesting: 38 of those 47 were add-ons to platforms that already exist. Nine were new platform creations — down from ten last year.

So the sponsors are not opening new fronts. They are thickening the ones they hold. Public strategics, meanwhile, doubled their participation, from five deals to eleven.

For anyone trying to start something rather than sell into something, that is the whole read: the competition for a first platform in an unassembled metro is thinner than the deal count suggests.

Full state-of-the-market assessment — six trades, the platform map, unit economics, and the metros still open — is on smbx.ai.

Figures: Capstone Partners, 27 July 2026.
