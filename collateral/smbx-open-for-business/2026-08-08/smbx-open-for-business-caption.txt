It is an entire corporate development function, without hiring one.

Here is a taste of the finding process. Last week, I read the Census County Business Patterns file and the Texas contractor license roster for the 11 counties of Dallas–Fort Worth. I wanted to know what a “saturated” metro is actually worth to a buyer.

Here is what I found:

• 2,412 plumbing and HVAC establishments.
• 1,797 of them employ fewer than ten people.
• 18 companies already own a business there.
• Roughly 280 businesses of buyable size are owned by none of them.

Nobody publishes that. You have to go and count it—and that is the part I enjoy most.
