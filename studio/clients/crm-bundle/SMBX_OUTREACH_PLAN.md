## The shape of the plan

Two research passes produced a target board and a capital-stack map. This turns both into a single operating sequence built around the only two hard dates on the calendar: **ACG DFW DealSource on September 3** and the **McGuireWoods Independent Sponsor Conference at the Fairmont Dallas on October 27–28**. Everything else is sequenced backward from those.

The organizing principle is that the capital layer gets worked before the client layer. Capital providers qualify which sponsors are real, they meet under-resourced buyers constantly, and a relationship there produces referrals for years. Going at operator-acquirers first would work, but it wastes the compounding.

**1. Register for both conferences this week.** McGuireWoods sponsorships for this cycle sold out early last time. Attendee registration should not be assumed open indefinitely, and it is the only event of the year where the primary target segment concentrates locally.

**2. Join ACG DFW before messaging anyone on the board.** Katherine Burrow is the membership chair and the first contact chronologically. Benjamin Riemer runs a 780-member chapter. Member status changes every subsequent conversation.

**3. Get Nate Christensen on the phone.** He chairs the ACG DFW Family Office Committee and advises PE sponsors and family offices on direct investments. He is the intro path to Rosewood, A.G. Hill, Hersh, and Lynch Holdings — four names that are otherwise very hard to reach cold. This is one contact that unlocks a whole segment.

**4. Finish the Fire & Life Safety DFW map before W1 opens.** Every W1 and W2 message offers it. Without it the outreach is a pitch, and with it the outreach is a delivery. Same words, entirely different reception.

**5. Finish the consolidators.md brand/DBA pass.** Until that lands, "independent" in the register means "unmatched to a known acquirer," not confirmed independent. That distinction will not survive a first meeting with anyone who knows the market, and Rosewood and National Fire & Safety both know the market.

## Guardrails encoded in every template

Buy-side only, never sell-side. Advisors, brokers, bankers and lenders are customers and referral sources — never competitors, never compared against. No fee or pricing content anywhere. No exclusivity or scarcity language; soft closes only. Track record framed as transactions led or co-led in the course of employment, never as smbX engagements. Analysis, options, implications — never recommend, negotiate on behalf, represent, or guarantee. No size ceiling implied and no "small business" framing.

One additional caution sits on REF-021: wealth-manager relationships drift toward sell-side referrals by gravity. Those get declined, warmly.
