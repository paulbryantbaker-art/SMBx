# Apex Service Partners — the DFW position, resolved as far as public sources allow

**Why this run existed.** Run 01 could not establish whether Apex holds ground in
DFW. Apex claims 75 brands across 46 states and publishes no roster. An unnamed
owned business is counted as an independent target, which is the error this
research exists to prevent.

## The finding, in two halves that must not be conflated

### Confirmed: Apex's corporate headquarters is now inside the MSA

The Apollo press release announcing the minority investment carries the dateline,
verbatim:

> "TAMPA, FL and DALLAS-FORT WORTH, TX — (May 28, 2026)"

Dallas Innovates, same date: *"Apex recently relocated its headquarters from
Tampa, Florida, to Dallas."* Employment listings for Apex in Texas resolve to
Irving, TX 75038 — Dallas County, inside the MSA — and are corporate functions
(tax, HRIS, payroll, people operations, AI tooling), not field or branch roles.

The release's own boilerplate, verbatim: *"the Company operates in 46 states with
+150 locations, +13,000 teammates and +$3B in annual revenue"*, and in the body,
*"the company operates 75 prominent local brands across 46 states, with more than
13,000 employees"*. **Neither statement names a metro.**

### Not found: any Apex operating brand in DFW, or anywhere in Texas

No press release, trade article, brand-site footer or sponsor portfolio page
identifies an Apex-owned HVAC, plumbing or electrical business in Texas. Every
Apex acquisition that could be dated lands outside the state — Southern Air
(Ball LA), Best Home Services (Naples FL), Frank Gay (Orlando FL), BelRed
(Seattle WA), Dilling (Charlotte NC), Haley Mechanical (Ann Arbor MI), Korte Does
It All (Fort Wayne IN), AB May (Kansas City MO), ProFlo and We Care (Temecula
CA), HomeBreeze (CA), Hansen Super Techs. The Frontier Service Partners platform
deal of 2024-01-24 brought three brands — Haley, Korte, AB May — all Midwest.

## This is absence of evidence, not evidence of absence

**The distinction decides how the number is used, so it is stated plainly.** No
complete Apex roster exists publicly, so nothing was found that *excludes* Texas.

The master already carries Apex brand counts as 19 publicly listed against 75
against 107, and instructs that they be presented as 75–107, platform-reported,
with directory lag noted. This run adds only that the identifiable share has not
improved: Apex's own site names three brands across its homepage, /who-we-are and
/why-apex, and has no brands, locations or portfolio page at all. The 107 figure
traces to a commercial aggregator and the 19 to a vendor-published ownership
tracker; both are low-grade sources and neither is a Census-style authority. They
are recorded here as the only quantifications available, not as verified figures.

**What follows for the study.** DFW HVAC independents cannot be treated as
Apex-free. A platform running 150-plus locations across 46 states, which moved
its corporate headquarters into this metro in 2026, is a high prior for local
ownership it has not published. The independent residual is therefore overstated
by an unknown quantity — **unknown, not zero, and not estimated.**

## Do not count these as Apex

Searching "Apex" against Texas HVAC surfaces several unrelated businesses: Apex
Luxury HVAC (Houston), Apex Heating & Air Conditioning (Austin), Apex Air
Electric (San Antonio), Apex Mechanical Group (North Texas), Apex Comfort
Solutions. **No evidence connects any of them to Apex Service Partners.** The
shared word is coincidence. Treating them as owned would be the mirror image of
the error this run was commissioned to close — a false *affiliated* that
suppresses a genuine target.

For the same reason `Apex` must not be added to the consolidator register as a
brand token. It is a common word and the matcher would false-match all five.

## What would actually resolve this

Named-brand matching has reached its ceiling on Apex. Three routes remain, none
of them public-web:

1. **TDLR licence records** — the air conditioning contractor file carries
   business name alongside the licensed individual. An Apex-affiliated entity
   operating in DFW must employ a licensed contractor at each permanent office
   (Tex. Occ. Code § 1302.252), so it is in that file under some name. See run 04.
2. **Texas Secretary of State entity search** — franchise-tax and registered-agent
   records for Apex-affiliated LLCs.
3. **County assumed-name (DBA) filings** in Dallas, Tarrant, Collin and Denton.

All three are entity-level lookups rather than searches, and they are the same
records a target hunt would use later.
