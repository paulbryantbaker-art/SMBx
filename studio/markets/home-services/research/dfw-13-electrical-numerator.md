**The finding is an absence, and it is stated as one.** Outside the multi-trade
home-services platforms and the franchise systems already in the register, **no
residential-electrical-first PE platform was found operating in DFW.** That is
absence of evidence from a searched field, not evidence of absence — but the
searched field included the sponsor wires, the platform rosters, and the obvious
query space, and it returned nothing.

Residential electrical in this metro is consolidated only three ways, all
already counted: Wrench Group's two brands (Berkeys and Baker Brothers both sell
electrical as a service line — plumbing/HVAC-led, not electrical-first), Mr.
Electric's ten Neighborly franchises, and Mister Sparky's two Authority Brands
franchises.

## What this means for the trade

DFW 238210 counts 1,634 establishments averaging 17.7 employees on $2.149B of
payroll (run 08) with a derived $6.880B of receipts (run 12) — a bigger-per-firm,
commercial-weighted trade in which **the residential service segment is
essentially unconsolidated**. Whether that is an opening or a warning depends on
why the platforms have not come: the code's 68.1% core is electric-power work
dominated by construction and industrial demand, and the residential service
annuity that drives HVAC consolidation is a far thinner slice here. That
question — is residential electrical service a consolidatable annuity in this
metro — is a thesis question, not a data question, and it is left open.
