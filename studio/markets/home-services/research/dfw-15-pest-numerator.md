# DFW pest control — the ownership picture

**The most consolidated trade in the metro, and the most active right now.**
Nine consolidator parents hold verified DFW ground, and three of the entries are
dated within the last twenty months: Barefoot's Euless tuck-in (2024-12-20),
Anticimex's three-company entry (2025-06-16), and Rollins' Romex acquisition
(2026-04-02).

## Notable independent

**ABC Home & Commercial Services** — est. 1949, self-described largest
independently owned pest company in Texas; DFW offices in Lewisville and Plano,
run by the founding family's third generation. A landmark independent, not a
consolidator — and precisely the profile a pest buy-side mandate would care
about. The ownership split between the DFW, Austin, Houston and San Antonio ABC
entities is not clarified on its pages.

## The read

Pest is the inverse of electrical: **396 establishments** (run 08) — the
smallest universe of the six trades — carrying at least **26 verified
platform-held locations across nine parents**, the highest platform density in
the study. It is also the one trade where the recurring-revenue annuity is
strongest (the master carries 85.4% residential recurring share, verified
2026-07-27), which is exactly why the consolidators got here first. For a
buy-side mandate the residual is thin and the competition thick — but the
dollar side can be **measured, not derived** at metro level once EC2256BASIC is
downloaded, which no construction trade allows.
