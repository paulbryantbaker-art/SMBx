# Run 13 — register contradictions, resolved

Six contradictions carried in `markets/elevator/screen/consolidators.md`
(`## Coverage and gaps` → *Live conflicts retained*), plus the three empty-domain
entries. Source priority applied throughout: **company's own site → sponsor's
portfolio page → PR wire → trade press → directory.**

**Law 1 governed the domain work.** No domain below was constructed or inferred
from a company name. Where a host could not be reached and confirmed, the field
stays EMPTY and the candidate is recorded in prose with its evidence, so the next
pass can close it rather than re-guess it. `integrityelevator.com` and
`elevatorserviceinc.com` were not revisited and are not resurrected.

## 1. TAKA Elevator — three attributions, one company, a sequence

**The three versions on file.** Run 04 credits "TAKA Elevator service (2019)" to
**Otis** in its A13–A16 strategic list, and in the same sentence notes the deal is
"listed under KONE in Maven's ledger." Runs 04b and 09b place **TAKA Elevator Co.,
LLC (Orlando FL)** under **TEI Group** at 2026-01-03.

**What the sources say.**

- **Maven Group's own transaction ledger** (mavenelevator.com/transactions) carries
  two separate TAKA rows: **2019 | seller "TAKA Elevator Company (Service)" | FL |
  buyer KONE Elevator**, and **2025 | seller Taka Elevator | FL | buyer TEI Group**.
  The ledger has **no Otis/TAKA row at all**. Otis's two rows are Urban Elevator
  Service (2025) and Elevator Solutions (2018).
- **TEI Group's own news item** (teigroup.com, Northeast region, article id=182),
  dated **January 3, 2025**: "TEI Group … is pleased to announce the acquisition of
  TAKA Elevator, an independent provider of elevator services based in Orlando,
  Florida." Legal name **TAKA Elevator Company, LLC**; established **2012**;
  founders **Patrick and Mary Lalchandani**, Patrick as President, retained under
  TEI's Southeastern Regional VP. TEI's news index lists the item under
  **January 3, 2025**.
- **Elevator World**, "TEI Group Acquires Orlando, Florida-based TAKA Elevator":
  "NYC-based TEI Group announced on January 3 it has acquired TAKA Elevator Co.,
  LLC."
- **PitchBook** (company 342138-43): TAKA Elevator Company "acquired on
  **03-Jan-2025**" by **Transel Elevator & Electric**, now an operating subsidiary —
  and, separately, "**Kone has invested in TAKA Elevator Company**." PitchBook
  records **no Otis relationship**.

**The chain, with dates.**

| Date | Event | Source |
|---|---|---|
| 2012 | TAKA Elevator Company, LLC founded, Orlando FL, by Patrick and Mary Lalchandani. Founder-owned. | TEI Group news, 2025-01-03; Elevator World |
| 2019 | **KONE** acquires the **service book** — Maven's ledger names the seller "TAKA Elevator Company (**Service**)", i.e. the maintenance portfolio, not the company | Maven ledger; corroborated in kind by PitchBook's KONE tie |
| 2025-01-03 | **TEI Group / Transel Elevator & Electric** acquires **TAKA Elevator Company, LLC** — the residual install, modernization and rebuilt-service business | TEI Group own news; Elevator World; PitchBook; Maven ledger |

So all three attributions are not equally true. **Two are true in sequence; the
Otis one is false.** A service book carved out and sold, then the company sold
seven years later, is exactly the "bought, carved out and resold" pattern — but the
2019 buyer was KONE, not Otis. Run 04's Otis line is an internal transcription
error against its own cited source, and it flagged the discrepancy in the same
sentence without resolving it.

**Two caveats that must travel with this.** The 2019 KONE service deal rests on
**one source** — Maven Group's ledger, the sell-side advisor's own record — plus a
PitchBook investor tag with no date or terms. No KONE press release for it was
located. It is good enough to remove Otis and to stop treating "TAKA" as a
screenable independent; it is not good enough to publish. And the **year on the TEI
deal in the register is wrong**: the register says 2026-01-03, four sources say
**2025-01-03**.

> **VERDICT: RESOLVED.** TAKA Elevator Company, LLC (Orlando FL, founded 2012,
> Lalchandani family) sold its **service portfolio to KONE in 2019** (Maven Group
> ledger; PitchBook records KONE as an investor), and the **company itself to TEI
> Group / Transel Elevator & Electric on 2025-01-03** (TEI's own news release,
> Elevator World, PitchBook, Maven ledger). **The Otis attribution is not supported
> by any source and is withdrawn** — Maven's ledger, the origin of the claim, has no
> Otis/TAKA row. Current owner of the TAKA name and entity: **TEI Group**.
> Correction to the register beyond the conflict itself: **the TEI acquisition date
> is 2025-01-03, not 2026-01-03.**

## 2. Century Elevator — Align Capital vs H.I.G. / Maven

**The two versions on file.** Align Capital Partners' exit release claims Century
among two add-ons completed during **its** hold; Maven's year-keyed ledger and run
04's table place Century under **H.I.G.** in 2024.

**What the sources say.**

- **Align Capital Partners**, "Align Capital Partners Completes Sale of Action
  Elevator" (aligncp.com), sale dated **September 3, 2024**: "Action completed two
  strategic acquisitions during its partnership with ACP, including **Avery Elevator
  Corporation and Century Elevator**, which expanded the Company's presence in the
  Mid-Atlantic."
- **H.I.G. Capital**, "H.I.G. Capital Completes Acquisition of Action Elevator"
  (hig.com), completion **September 3, 2024**: "**Concurrent with this transaction**,
  Action acquired **Century Elevator**, a leading provider of elevator services in
  the Washington D.C. market."
- **Maven Group**, "Action Elevator Strengthens Its Mid-Atlantic Portfolio by
  Acquiring Century Elevator", dated **September 3, 2024**: acquirer **Action
  Elevator**; Century serves the greater Washington D.C. market across **MD, DC and
  VA**; sellers are Century's **founders Charlie and Clarisse Choux**; the sponsor
  named as backing Action is **H.I.G. Capital**.
- **PitchBook** (company 448694-20): Century Elevator, **founded 1989**,
  **Forestville, MD** (6801 Marlboro Pike); acquired by Action Elevator Company
  **03-Sep-2024**; **no prior institutional owner or sponsor** on record.

**Resolution.** There is no ownership conflict. Both releases describe **the same
single event on the same single day**: Century Elevator was acquired by the Action
Elevator platform on **2024-09-03**, the day the platform itself passed from Align
Capital Partners to H.I.G. Capital. Align counts it inside its hold because it
closed as part of the exit; H.I.G. calls it "concurrent" because it closed
alongside its entry. Neither is wrong, and the two do not describe two Centurys.

> **VERDICT: RESOLVED.**
> **Current owner: Action Elevator (platform), sponsored by H.I.G. Capital since
> 2024-09-03** (hig.com completion release; Maven, 2024-09-03).
> **Prior owner: Century Elevator's founders, Charlie and Clarisse Choux — the
> company was founder-owned from its founding in 1989 until 2024-09-03** (Maven;
> PitchBook). It was **never** separately held by Align Capital Partners. Align was
> the prior sponsor **of the Action platform** (2019-09 – 2024-09-03), not of
> Century.
> Detail closed while resolving: Century is **Forestville, MD**, founded **1989**,
> serving **MD, DC, VA**.

## 3. Elevated Holdings — three footprint versions

**The three versions on file.** 21 states / 57 markets (own site, 2026) · 22 states
/ 30+ markets (APi acquisition materials, 2024) · 18+ states (Elevator World). The
register reads the 22→21 as possible contraction and does not assert it.

**What the sources say — and each is a different date.**

| As of | Footprint | Source |
|---|---|---|
| 2023-10-23 | "**30+ markets in 22 states**" | Elevated's own rebrand release, PR Newswire, 2023-10-23 — the release that consolidated **EMR Elevator, Oracle Elevator, Premier Elevator and Premier-Oracle** under one brand |
| **2024-06-04 (close)** | "serves customers in **over 18 states**", "**approximately 600 leaders**", "**approximately $220 million in annual revenue**", HQ **Tampa, Florida** | **APi Group's own completion release**, ir.apigroupcorp.com, dated 06/04/2024 |
| 2026 | "**57 markets across 21 states**", HQ 8800 Grand Oak Circle #550, Tampa FL 33637 | elevatedfacilityservices.com, current |

The APi announcement release of **2024-04-15** (BusinessWire) carries the price —
"**approximately $570 million in cash**" — and "**~$220 million in annual
revenue**", but **states no state or market count at all**. The "22 states / 30+
markets" figure is therefore **not** from APi's acquisition materials as the
register records it; it is **Elevated's own October 2023 rebrand release**, seven
and a half months before close. Elevator World's "18+ states" is trade press
restating APi's own "over 18 states."

**Resolution.** These are three dated snapshots, not three claims about one date —
and two of the three do not even conflict: "**over 18 states**" is a floor, and 22
clears it. The apparent 22→21 contraction is an artifact of comparing a 2023
marketing release to a 2026 website. The markets figure 30+ → 57 across roughly
two and a half years, against a state count that moves by at most one, does point
to a change in counting convention rather than to expansion into 27 new markets —
but no source states the convention, and it is **not asserted**.

> **VERDICT: RESOLVED for the date that matters.**
> **Footprint as of the APi Group close on 4 June 2024, from APi's own completion
> release: over 18 states, approximately 600 employees, approximately $220 million
> annual revenue, headquartered in Tampa, Florida** (ir.apigroupcorp.com,
> 06/04/2024). Purchase price approximately **$570 million cash** (BusinessWire,
> 2024-04-15).
> **Changed since:** the company's own site now states **57 markets across 21
> states** (2026) and no longer surfaces the legacy operating brands.
> **Correction to the register:** the "22 states / 30+ markets" figure is
> **Elevated's own rebrand release of 2023-10-23**, not APi's 2024 acquisition
> materials — APi's materials give no state count. Re-dated, and the "contraction"
> reading is withdrawn as an artifact of comparing different dates.
> **Still unknown, and left so:** no current unit count. The 11,000+ remains stale
> from the Incline hold.

## 5. Standard Elevator Systems — McIntosh Industries, and the domain

### 5a. The McIntosh relationship

The register retains the conflict correctly: Arcline's platform-formation release
of **2022-01-26** (PR Newswire) names **five** suppliers acquired simultaneously —
**Standard Elevator Systems, LLC** (Memphis TN) · **EMI Porta, LLC** (Arlington
Heights IL) · **ZZIPCO, LLC** (Franklin Lakes NJ) · **Texacone, LLC** (Mesquite TX)
· **Elevator Equipment Company, LLC / EECO** (Los Angeles CA, plus a Richmond IN
facility) — and **McIntosh is not among them**. The release's New Jersey entity is
ZZIPCO.

The relationship is a **later add-on**, and it is documented:

- **Elevator World**, "SES Acquires McIntosh Industries Inc.": "Standard Elevator
  Systems (SES), which manufactures and provides products and solutions for
  elevators, escalators and moving walks" acquired **McIntosh Industries Inc.** of
  **Hillside, New Jersey**, announced **June 23**.
- **Maven Group**, "Standard Elevator Systems Completes Acquisition of McIntosh
  Industries Inc.", dated **June 23, 2022**: McIntosh is "the trusted name in the
  rebuilding, repair and service of **elevator motors and machines**", over 30 years
  in business; SES headquartered **Memphis, Tennessee**.
- **Bass, Berry & Sims** (deal counsel), "Standard Elevator Systems Acquires
  McIntosh Industries, LLC": SES described as an **Arcline Investment Management**
  portfolio company; McIntosh "a company specializing in rebuilding, repair and
  service of elevator motors and machines"; terms not disclosed.
- **Standard Elevator Systems' own LinkedIn** post announcing the acquisition
  timestamps to **2022-06-23**.

One residual wrinkle, recorded and not resolved because nothing turns on it for
screening: the entity suffix differs by source — **"McIntosh Industries Inc."**
(Elevator World, Maven, the company's own post) vs **"McIntosh Industries, LLC"**
(Bass Berry, counsel on the deal). Counsel is usually right about a legal name, but
the majority read "Inc."; both are noted so a company-string match fires either way.

> **VERDICT: RESOLVED. McIntosh Industries (Hillside, NJ — elevator motor and
> machine rebuilding, repair and service) is a LATER ADD-ON, acquired by Standard
> Elevator Systems on 2022-06-23** — five months after the platform was formed on
> 2022-01-26 — **not one of the five founding suppliers, and not a
> mis-attribution.** Sources: Maven Group 2022-06-23; Elevator World; Bass, Berry &
> Sims (deal counsel); SES's own LinkedIn announcement. Both name forms retained
> (Inc. per three sources, LLC per counsel). The five founding suppliers stand
> exactly as Arcline's 2022-01-26 release names them.

### 5b. The domain — NOT closed, and left empty

A different candidate surfaced, **`standard-elevator.com`** (hyphenated), from two

  website as **www.standard-elevator.com** in a release about Standard Elevator
  Systems of **Memphis, Tennessee**.
- **CB Insights** ties `standard-elevator.com` to **4949 E. Raines Rd, Suite 101,
  Memphis, Tennessee 38118**.
- The search index returns live pages on that host whose titles self-identify —
  `standard-elevator.com/about-us/` → "Standard Elevator Systems – Solutions for the
  Field"; `standard-elevator.com/technical-support/contact-us/` → "Technical Support
  Contact – Standard Elevator Systems". A `brasil.standard-elevator.com` subdomain
  also exists.
- DNS resolves: `standard-elevator.com` and `www.standard-elevator.com` →
  44.212.220.145.

**But the host could not be reached.** Every fetch attempt — root, `/about-us/`,
`/technical-support/contact-us/`, and the Brazilian subdomain — failed at the
robots.txt stage with connect timeouts and a certificate error. **The site was
never reached and never confirmed itself as this company to me.** Arcline's own
release names no URL, and Arcline's portfolio page does not carry Standard Elevator
at all.

Law 1's test is *reached and confirmed*, and this fails it. Recording it in the
`domains:` field on a directory entry and an advisor's press boilerplate would put
an unverified host into the one field a screener uses to decide ownership.

> **VERDICT: UNRESOLVED — domain field stays EMPTY.** `standard-elevator.com` is a
> **strong candidate, not a confirmation**: named by Maven Group (2022-06-23) and CB
> Insights against the Memphis TN address, with live self-identifying page titles in
> the search index and clean DNS — but the host would not serve robots.txt to the
> fetcher on four attempts, so it was never reached. It is recorded in the note line
> with its evidence so the next pass can close it in one fetch. **It is not written
> into `domains:` and it must not be treated as establishing ownership until someone
> loads the page.** Screen this entity by full company string plus state, as before.

## Other things that looked wrong while I was in there

Recorded, not all actioned.

1. **The TEI/TAKA date is off by a year — actioned.** The register says
   **2026-01-03**; TEI's own news item, its news index, Elevator World, PitchBook
   and Maven's ledger all put it at **2025-01-03**. This matters beyond tidiness:
   the register's TEI entry reads as a live 2026 buyer, and a "last deal" freshness
   check off this row would score TEI a year newer than it is. Corrected.
2. **"22 states / 30+ markets" is mis-attributed — actioned.** The register credits
   it to "APi acquisition materials, 2024." It is Elevated's **own rebrand release
   of 2023-10-23**. APi's announcement and completion releases give **no** state or
   market count; the completion release gives "over 18 states." Re-attributed and
   re-dated.
3. **LiftNet may sit under two Arcline entries at once — flagged only.** The
   register places LiftNet under **Kings III** (acquired 2026-04-02). Maven's
   2022-06-23 Standard Elevator Systems item lists LiftNet among **SES's** brands.
   Both are Arcline holdings, so an intra-sponsor transfer would explain it — but
   nothing on file establishes that, and a LiftNet match currently resolves to two
   parents. **Not resolved this pass; not asserted; worth one fetch next pass.**
4. **Elevator Systems, Inc. can now carry states — actioned.** Its own site names
   **Garden City, NY** manufacturing and a **Sunrise, FL** warehouse, closing a
   field that was wholly empty. It also dates the company to **1973**.
5. **Century Elevator's own detail was never in the register — actioned.**
   Forestville, MD; founded 1989; founder-owned by Charlie and Clarisse Choux until
   2024-09-03.
6. **The register's "26 parents" is consistent** — 10 + 4 + 7 + 5 = 26. Checked
   because the class table is the kind of thing that drifts silently. It has not.
7. **Not chased, and still open:** the Otis 2019 TAKA line existed for four days
   inside a source file that flagged its own contradiction in the same sentence and
   carried it forward anyway. That is the failure mode worth naming — the conflict
   was **visible at the point of writing** and was still promoted into a derived
   register. The register's "live conflicts retained" section is what caught it,
   which is an argument for keeping that section rather than resolving it away.
