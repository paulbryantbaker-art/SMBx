## The coverage number, which is the finding

`sourcing-protection.mts` classifies every origin in the corpus and refuses to
verdict below a **90%** floor, because a collapse test is silent on a domain it
cannot class and at scale that silence becomes the result.

On first run this market's corpus classified at **40%** of 409 origins — the
corpus every previous "clean" elevator sourcing result was judged against.
Those results were issued blind.

After classing this market's domains by hand — the OEMs and their filings
pages, the sponsors, the operators, the associations, and the federal
statistical publishers that do not sit on a `.gov` host — coverage reached
**96%**, and the sighted run reported **13 findings** that no earlier run could
have seen.

None of the thirteen is a wrong figure. Each is a statement about the class of
source a figure terminates at: how many citations, how many distinct origins,
and whether any of them is a statute, a filing or a government table.
