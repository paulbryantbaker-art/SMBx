# Studio addendum — harvested method, 2026-08-17

Studio-side takings from the four downloaded skill sets. These are **method fragments to
fold into the studio's own docs** (PLAYBOOK.md / RESEARCH.md / FORMATS.md), not skills to
install — the studio already has its law; this sharpens three edges of it. File via a
studio session with smbx-studio preflight.

## 1. Market sizing cross-checks (→ RESEARCH.md)

Additions to the verification law for market masters and buy-boxes:

- **Substitution test for market definition.** Before sizing, define the market by what
  a buyer would use if the subject category didn't exist. One sentence, and it anchors
  all sizing. Kills the inflated-TAM problem at the definition stage.
- **Top-down / bottom-up within 3x.** Every market size gets both builds: top-down from
  a named, attributable source; bottom-up from establishment count × realistic spend.
  Divergence beyond ~3x means the definition or an assumption is wrong — investigate
  before the figure enters a master. (This is the same discipline as the audit's
  figure-must-trace rule, applied to derivation instead of citation.)
- **Growth ceiling check.** Project any claimed growth rate 5 years forward: if it
  implies >30–40% penetration of the serviceable market, the claim requires market
  expansion, which changes the risk profile. Flag it in the master.
- **Base-rate test.** A projected rate materially above the historical rate requires a
  named structural change. No name, no credit.

## 2. Screening additions (→ PLAYBOOK.md, screen/ conventions)

- **Angle + objection per priority target.** Register rows for priority-tier candidates
  carry two extra fields: the tailored approach angle, and the likely objection with
  its answer. Converts a list into an outreach plan.
- **Why-now line in every thesis.** Each investment thesis states the timing catalyst
  in one sentence. A thesis without a why-now is a watch-list entry, not a thesis.
- **Traffic-light quality scorecard.** Screen-stage candidates get green/amber/red on:
  revenue quality, customer concentration, margin vs trade norm, owner dependence
  signals, competitive moat — one evidence line each. Cheap at screen stage, and it
  pre-loads the deal-side work if the candidate is promoted at IoI.

## 3. Narrative rules (→ FORMATS.md / house voice)

Three tests, not new formats — the house system already owns layout and voice:

- **SCQA opener.** Reports and market assessments open with
  situation → complication → question, so the reader arrives at the document's
  governing question feeling it is inevitable.
- **Spine test.** Read only the titles/captions of a deck or carousel top to bottom:
  they must form the complete argument alone. If a title is a label ("Market
  Overview"), it fails; rewrite as the takeaway.
- **Approve-from-this-page test.** Any executive summary (report, one-pager, memo)
  must let a reader make the decision from that page alone: recommendation first and
  largest, then the two or three pillars, then the explicit ask.
