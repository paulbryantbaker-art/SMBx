HOW I UNDERWRITE
I ask three questions at day four.
About 47% of broken post-LOI deals now die on diligence findings — and the findings were almost always visible before anybody signed.
1
Do the add-backs survive a QoE?

Not “are they explained”. Do they survive somebody hostile with the ledger open.

2
Does revenue survive the owner’s exit?

Name the accounts that leave in the same car, and price the deal without them.

3
Is the peg built on actuals?

A peg assembled from the seller’s normalisation is a number you renegotiate at close, from the weaker position.

Source: Axial Dead Deal Report, 75 transactions, 2025 — member-reported; Axial sells access to the channel it measures.
