# Gated collateral

PDFs here are deliberately OUTSIDE client/public: they are delivered by
email through POST /api/practice/pricing (lead captured first), never by
a guessable URL. Drop the pricing brochure here as
`smbx-corpdev-pricing.pdf` — the route picks it up by that exact name.
The free offering brochure (no pricing) stays a public download at
client/public/collateral/smbx-corpdev-offering.pdf.
